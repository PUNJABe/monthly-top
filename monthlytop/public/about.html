is a public leaderboard for websites, profiles, apps and AI tools. Anyone can list a link, and rank is what you pay: no ads, no votes, no revenue share. Just claim #1.

Why it exists

Most rankings are decided by algorithms or editors. This one is decided by how much people are willing to put behind a link, and every payment is public on the listing's page. Each month the chart resets, so there is always a fresh shot at the top.

How it works

  1. Paste a link. A website, or a profile, channel, repository, app or AI model page on GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, X and more.
  2. Say what it is. Add a name and a one-line description, then pick a category. If yours is missing, create it.
  3. Boost it. Choose an amount in rupees and pay securely. Your boost is added to the listing's total.
  4. Climb the chart. Listings are ranked by total money paid, on the main chart and inside their category. At month end the chart resets and the winners go to Past winners.
