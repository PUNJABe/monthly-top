A boost is a payment for ranking a listing on this chart and is generally final once the payment is complete.

We will refund when

  • You were charged more than once for the same boost by mistake.
  • The wrong listing was boosted because of an error on our side.
  • We remove a listing for breaking our rules and the removal was not caused by the person who paid.
  • The law in your country gives you a right to a refund.

How to ask

Use the contact form within 14 days with the email you used, the listing and the amount. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and typically appear in 5 to 10 business days. A refunded boost is removed from the chart.
