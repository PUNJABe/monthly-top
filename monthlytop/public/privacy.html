What we collect

  • When you boost: your email address, the display name and message you choose to enter, the amount, and (for a new listing) the link, name, description and category you submit. Your email is used for payment confirmation and support and is never shown publicly.
  • Payment details: collected and processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Contact form: your name, email and message.
  • Technical data: your IP address is processed briefly for rate limiting and security.
  • Visitor numbers: we count visitors with DataFast analytics, or with our own cookieless counter that stores only a daily, non-reversible hash.

What is public

A listing's link, name, description and category, plus the display name (or "Anonymous"), message and amount of each boost, are public. Do not enter anything you want to keep private.

Logos

Logos and avatars are loaded from third-party services such as Google's favicon service and GitHub. When a page with a logo loads, your browser contacts those services and they can see your IP address and which site's logo was requested.

Why we use it

To process payments, run the chart, prevent fraud and abuse, answer support requests and meet legal obligations such as tax records.

Sharing

We share data only with Stripe (payments), our hosting provider, DataFast (if enabled) and the logo services above. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and your rights

We keep payment records as long as the law requires. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to processing. Contact us to make a request.

Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. Stripe may set cookies during checkout, and DataFast sets its own if enabled.
